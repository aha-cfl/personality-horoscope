Where you likely stand, and how sure we are

Each band is the range your true score probably falls in, at 90% confidence. A narrow band means the scale measured you precisely. A wide band means it could not. The dot is your best single estimate; the band is the honest answer.

What if the scales were more reliable?

Reliability (Cronbach’s α) sets the band width: SEM = SD·√(1−α). Drag to see how much precision a longer or better scale would buy.
